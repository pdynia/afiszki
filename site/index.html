About us

A bridge between European quality and regional demand.

Halico Trading is a Dubai-based trading company specialising in the import and distribution of food supplements produced in the European Union.

Dubai's location and logistics infrastructure let us hold stock close to our customers and ship quickly to markets across the Gulf, the Levant and North Africa. We handle the sourcing, documentation and logistics, so our partners can focus on selling.

What we do

From the manufacturer to your shelf.

  1. 01

    Sourcing in Europe

    We work directly with European manufacturers and brand owners to select food supplements suited to regional markets.

  2. 02

    Import & documentation

    We manage shipping, customs clearance and the product documentation needed for registration in each destination country.

  3. 03

    Dubai warehouse

    Products are stored in our warehouse in Dubai, ready for fast dispatch in quantities that fit your orders.

  4. 04

    Regional distribution

    We supply pharmacies, retail chains, e-commerce sellers and local distributors across the MENA region.
